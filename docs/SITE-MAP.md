# Site Map and Page Structure

## Recommended routes

- `/` — Home
- `/services` — Services overview
- `/services/shower-enclosures` — Shower Enclosures
- `/services/glass-railings` — Glass Railings
- `/services/custom-mirrors` — Custom Mirrors
- `/gallery` — Completed Projects
- `/about` — About LA Glass
- `/contact` — Contact and Free Quote
- `/privacy` — Privacy Policy

Create only the pages that have enough truthful content. A well-built smaller site is better than thin pages filled with invented copy.

## Home page order

1. Header
2. Hero video or poster fallback
3. Short trust/value strip
4. Services overview
5. Featured projects
6. Why choose LA Glass
7. Installation process
8. Verified statistics, only after confirmation
9. Authentic reviews, only after they are supplied
10. Los Angeles service-area section
11. FAQ
12. Final quote call to action
13. Footer

## Header

Desktop:

- Logo
- Home
- Services
- Gallery
- About
- Contact
- Call button or Request Quote button

Mobile:

- Logo
- Accessible menu button
- Clear navigation drawer
- Prominent call/quote action
- Escape-key and outside-click closing behavior
- Proper focus management

## Services page

Include:

- Service introduction
- Service cards
- Detailed sections for each confirmed service
- Project imagery
- Process summary
- FAQ
- Quote call to action

## Service-detail pages

Each page should include:

- Clear service-specific hero
- What the service is
- Available configurations, only where confirmed
- Benefits
- Project examples
- Installation process
- Related FAQ
- Quote call to action
- Links to related services

## Gallery

- Real project images only
- Categories: Showers, Railings, Mirrors, and All
- Responsive grid
- Accessible lightbox or project-detail interaction
- Descriptive alt text
- Do not claim project locations unless supplied
- Avoid loading every full-resolution image immediately

## About

Include:

- Real company story, once supplied
- Verified experience
- Standards of workmanship
- Los Angeles service focus
- Genuine work/process imagery
- No invented team members

## Contact / Free Quote

Include:

- Quote form
- Phone
- Email
- Service area
- Business hours
- Social links
- Helpful response expectation only if confirmed
- Privacy notice near submission

## Footer

Include:

- Logo and short description
- Main navigation
- Confirmed services
- Confirmed contact information
- Social links
- Service-area statement
- Privacy link
- Copyright with current year
