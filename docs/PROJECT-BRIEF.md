# Project Brief

## Business

**Name:** LA Glass  
**Industry:** Custom glass installation  
**Primary service area:** Los Angeles area, California  
**Primary goal:** Generate qualified phone calls and quote requests from homeowners, contractors, designers, property managers, and businesses seeking custom glass installation.

## What LA Glass does

Confirmed service categories currently include:

- Custom glass installation
- Frameless shower enclosures
- Shower doors and related shower-glass systems
- Glass railings
- Custom mirrors

Do not add unrelated services without approval.

## Website objectives

1. Immediately communicate what LA Glass installs and where it works.
2. Present real completed work in a premium visual style.
3. Make requesting a quote fast and easy.
4. Build trust using only verified claims and authentic reviews.
5. Perform well on mobile devices.
6. Support local Los Angeles search visibility.
7. Make future edits and additions straightforward.

## Audience

- Los Angeles homeowners
- Home remodelers
- General contractors
- Interior designers
- Architects
- Property managers
- Commercial clients only where the requested service is confirmed

## Desired feeling

The website should feel:

- Precise
- Architectural
- Clean
- Reliable
- Modern
- Premium
- Experienced
- Local to Los Angeles

It should not feel:

- Flashy for no reason
- Overcrowded
- Like a generic AI template
- Like a luxury-fashion website unrelated to construction
- Difficult to navigate
- Slow because of unnecessary animation

## Primary calls to action

- Request a Free Quote
- Call LA Glass
- View Our Work
- Send Project Details

## Hero direction

A hero video will be supplied later.

Preferred AI-video concept:

- A modern bathroom or architectural setting begins with separated glass components.
- Tempered-glass panels, hinges, channels, handle, and hardware move smoothly into place.
- The completed shower enclosure is revealed.
- Movement is elegant and physically believable.
- Camera motion is subtle.
- The sequence loops cleanly.
- Important text remains readable.
- No text, labels, logos, people, or impossible hardware inside the generated footage.

Alternative concepts:

1. Empty bathroom transforming into a finished frameless shower.
2. Glass-railing components assembling along a staircase.
3. A close-up light reflection moving across glass, revealing several completed projects through seamless transitions.

The site must work before the final video arrives by using a poster image or temporary visual fallback.
